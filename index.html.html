Keys: ←/→ turn · ↑ descend / ↓ climb · 0 level off · A/Z speed · [ ] course (CRS) · , . heading bug · F type frequency (Enter to tune) · Space pause · 1/2/4 sim speed · N new run · M switch Approach/SID mode · D flight director on/off · T hold timer. Mouse wheel: drag the CRS / HDG knobs in a circle (or scroll over them), and scroll over the IAS or Climb/Desc buttons, to change them (up = increase, Shift = bigger steps: CRS/BUG ±5°, IAS ±10 kt, V/S ±500 fpm; one notch = CRS/BUG 1°, IAS 5 kt, V/S 100 fpm). Altitude alerts (above field): 1000, 500, 400, 300, 200 (minimums), 100, 50, 40, 30, 20 and 10 ft sound a callout (with chimes down to 100 ft); the Alerts button mutes them. Wind card: DIR/SPD buttons (scroll too), Random or Calm; a manual setting is kept for the next approaches, Random re-rolls each new approach. Over the HSI it turns CRS (Shift = bug), and over the frequency it tunes.

⚠ Training aid, not a professional simulator

This tool is a simplified training aid. It is not a certified or professional flight simulator.

Its purpose is to help you understand the basics of instrument flying and to help you stay proficient at following the needles: tuning the navigation radio, setting the course, reading the HSI and the localizer / glideslope indications, and flying a stable approach or a simple departure.

Aircraft handling, physics, wind and procedures are deliberately simplified, and the airports and procedures are fictional. Never use it for real navigation, and never as a substitute for approved training, an instructor, or official charts and procedures.
